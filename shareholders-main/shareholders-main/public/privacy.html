Privacy Policy

JTGC Shareholders app — Jeddah Tourist Guides Cooperative Association (JTGC).
Last updated: 28 June 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Jeddah Tourist Guides Cooperative Association ("JTGC", "we") collects, uses, retains, and protects your personal data in the JTGC Shareholders mobile application (the "App"). The App is a members-only portal for shareholders of the cooperative; accounts are provisioned by JTGC and accessed using your National ID and a one-time verification code.

1. Data we collect

We use Google Analytics (Firebase Analytics) to understand how the app is used — for example which screens are viewed — so we can improve it. This includes device model, app version, and an approximate (city/region-level) location inferred from your IP address; it is not linked to advertising. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or photos, we do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How we use your data

Solely to operate the shareholder portal: to verify your identity and log you in, to show your shareholding information, to send a one-time code by SMS or email, to deliver notifications you have permitted (e.g. new announcements, dividends), and to respond to your contact messages and feedback.

3. Legal basis

We process your data to perform our membership relationship with you and to comply with the cooperative's legal and regulatory record-keeping obligations, and—where applicable—based on your consent (which you give at sign-in and can withdraw).

4. Sharing & service providers

We do not sell your data. We use the following processors strictly to run the service:

  • Supabase — secure database, authentication, and storage hosting.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.
  • Google Analytics (Firebase Analytics) — usage analytics to improve the app.
  • Twilio / email (SMTP) — sending one-time verification codes.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access is restricted by row-level security so each member can only see their own records.

5. Data retention

As a cooperative, we are required to retain shareholder and financial records for legal, audit, and regulatory purposes. When you request account deletion (see §7) your account is deactivated and flagged for deletion, but membership records may be retained as required by law. You can request full erasure of data that is not subject to a retention obligation by contacting us.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent. Contact us at support@jtgc.sa.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete my account in the App. This deactivates your account and records a deletion request; the cooperative retains the membership records it is legally required to keep, and an administrator can restore the account on request. To request deletion outside the App, or to request erasure of non-retained data, email support@jtgc.sa. See also our dedicated account & data deletion page.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures: TLS encryption in transit, row-level security, one-time-code authentication, and optional biometric/PIN app lock. Session tokens are stored in the device's secure storage (Keychain/Keystore).

9. Children

The App is for adult shareholders and is not directed to children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with an updated date.

11. Contact

Jeddah Tourist Guides Cooperative Association — support@jtgc.sa.

٢. كيف نستخدم بياناتك

فقط لتشغيل بوابة المساهمين: التحقق من هويتك وتسجيل دخولك، وعرض بيانات مساهمتك، وإرسال رمز التحقق عبر الرسائل القصيرة أو البريد، وإرسال الإشعارات التي وافقت عليها، والرد على رسائلك وملاحظاتك.

٣. الأساس النظامي

نعالج بياناتك لتنفيذ علاقة العضوية معك وللامتثال لالتزامات حفظ السجلات النظامية للجمعية، وعند الاقتضاء بناءً على موافقتك التي يمكنك سحبها.

٤. المشاركة ومزودو الخدمة

لا نبيع بياناتك. نستخدم مزودي الخدمة التاليين لتشغيل الخدمة فقط: Supabase (قاعدة البيانات والمصادقة)، وGoogle Firebase (الإشعارات)، وGoogle Analytics (تحليلات الاستخدام لتحسين التطبيق)، وTwilio/البريد (رموز التحقق). البيانات مشفّرة أثناء النقل والوصول محصور بحيث يرى كل عضو سجلاته فقط.

٥. الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

تلتزم الجمعية بالاحتفاظ بسجلات المساهمين والسجلات المالية لأغراض نظامية وتدقيقية. عند طلب حذف الحساب يتم تعطيل حسابك وتسجيل طلب الحذف، مع الاحتفاظ بسجلات العضوية المطلوبة نظامًا.

٦. حذف الحساب

يمكنك حذف حسابك في أي وقت من الإعدادات ← حذف حسابي. يؤدي ذلك إلى تعطيل الحساب وتسجيل طلب الحذف؛ ويمكن للمشرف استعادته عند الحاجة. للطلب خارج التطبيق راسلنا على support@jtgc.sa.

٧. حقوقك والتواصل

لك حق الوصول إلى بياناتك وتصحيحها وطلب حذفها وسحب الموافقة. للتواصل: support@jtgc.sa.
